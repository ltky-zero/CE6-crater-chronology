# Provenance

## Data produced in this study

- Five SCC files: crater catalogues and counting-area polygons manually mapped
  from the Chang'e-2 CE2TMap2015 orthophoto and exported by CraterTools.
- Regional histogram summaries: computed from the processed FeO, TiO2, Mg#, and
  spectral-parameter rasters described in the manuscript and Supporting
  Information.
- CE6_L-inclusive chronology fits, residual tables, and sampled curve coordinates.
- Figure-generation code and exported figure files.

## Third-party inputs

- Chang'e-2 CE2TMap2015 orthophoto: Li et al. (2018).
- Lunar compositional products: Lemelin et al. (2015), Sato et al. (2017), and
  Qiu et al. (2025), as specified in the manuscript.
- Published calibration ages and crater densities: the sources named in each
  calibration CSV and in the manuscript reference list.
- Cui chronology input verification: Xiao (2024), Zenodo DOI
  10.5281/zenodo.13989416, supporting Cui et al. (2024).

The third-party source rasters and source publications are cited but are not
redistributed in this package. No source SCC file or numerical calibration value
has been altered merely for packaging.
